## What failed or remains limited

- The tested Capterra category page timed out.
- A successful listicle capture does not prove a submission route, free inclusion, or editorial value.
- A truncated capture is `unknown`, not evidence that a brand is absent.
- Search results are discovery leads only; the page body supplies presence evidence.

Review each directory's current terms and submission rules before collection or manual action. No form was submitted and no route was followed during the smoke check.
